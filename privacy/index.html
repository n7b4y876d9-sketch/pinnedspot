PinnedSpot

Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026, the day PinnedSpot was first made available to testers through TestFlight.

PinnedSpot reminds you when you get near a place you saved. It was built so that your places stay yours. This page says what the app stores, what it sends, and to whom.

The short version

  • PinnedSpot has no accounts and no server of its own. The app does not send us your saved places, your notes or your location.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • Two things go online, only when you use them: searching for a place, and showing a map.
  • We receive something from you only if you choose to send it, such as feedback or a screenshot.

What stays on your device

Your saved places (their names, locations, reminder distances and any notes), your settings, and the details PinnedSpot needs to run your reminders are stored on your device and encrypted there. PinnedSpot does not upload them to us or to anyone else.

Your phone, not a server, notices when you arrive: PinnedSpot asks iOS to watch the areas around your saved places, and iOS tells the app when you enter one. To choose which saved places to watch, PinnedSpot reads your location once and does not save it. PinnedSpot keeps no history of where you go.

Reminders are notifications created on your device. PinnedSpot does not use a push-notification service to send them.

What goes online, and to whom

Searching for a place. When you search for an address or a place name, the text you type and your internet (IP) address are sent to LocationIQ, the service that looks places up, so it can return results. PinnedSpot sends no account, name or device identifier with a search. LocationIQ handles that request under its own privacy policy: locationiq.com/privacy.

Showing a map. When the app shows a map of a place, your device asks Apple Maps to draw that area. That tells Apple roughly where the place is, or where you are if you are saving your current location. Apple handles that request under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

PinnedSpot does not send your saved places or notes to either service.

Things your device controls

  • Backups. If you back up your device, the backup may include PinnedSpot's data. Whether and where your device is backed up is set in your device's settings, not in PinnedSpot.
  • Notifications on other devices. If you have set your phone to show notifications on a paired device, such as a watch, PinnedSpot's reminders can appear there too. PinnedSpot's "Private notifications" setting hides place names in reminders.
  • Permissions. PinnedSpot asks for location access, including "Always", so reminders work when the app is closed, and for permission to send notifications. You can change either in Settings at any time; reminders stop working without them.

What you choose to send us

Everything above is about what the app does on its own. It is different when you decide to send us something:

  • Feedback and support. If you write to us, or send feedback or a screenshot, we receive what you sent and the address it came from. A screenshot shows whatever was on your screen, which can include a saved place, its location or a note.
  • Test versions. If you use a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple provides us with crash reports, your device model and iOS version, and any feedback or screenshots you submit. If you were invited by email, we can see the email address the invitation was sent to. Apple describes this in its TestFlight terms.

We use what you send only to answer you and to fix and improve PinnedSpot. We keep it only as long as that takes, we do not sell or share it, and we will delete it if you ask.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved place in the app. "Delete all local data" in Settings removes everything PinnedSpot has stored on your device, including the keys that protect it.

Deleting the app removes the app and the files it stored. iOS can, however, keep an app's keychain entries after the app is deleted, and PinnedSpot keeps its encryption keys there. Those keys are not your places or notes, but if you want them gone too, use "Delete all local data" before you delete the app.

The app does not send us your saved places, notes or location, so we hold none of them to delete. Anything you chose to send us, such as feedback, we will delete on request. Copies in a device backup you made earlier are governed by that backup.

Children

PinnedSpot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has sent us something, write to us and we will delete it.
