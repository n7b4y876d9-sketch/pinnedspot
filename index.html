PinnedSpot

Reminders when you get near a place you saved.

PinnedSpot is an iPhone app. Save a store, a school or a friend's house, choose how close you want the reminder, and your phone notifies you when you arrive, even if the app is closed.

Your places are stored on your phone. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

PinnedSpot is in testing and is not yet on the App Store.
